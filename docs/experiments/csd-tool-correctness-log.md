**Symptom / question:** the four tool defects (duplicate-decoded-string masking, GetTopKTokens unsatisfiable contract, GetLogitGap mask-skip mismatch, ConstrainedSymbol accepting dead-end prefixes) were fixed. Do the fixes (a) do what they are intended to do, and (b) leave the four accepted held-out winners unchanged?

- B1 behavioral: a `ConstrainedSymbol` call on a chunk that ends in a dead-end prefix now STOPS at the last live prefix instead of accepting the dead one (observable: returned symbol length / accepted-prefix differs between pre-fix and post-fix logic on a constructed dead-end case).
